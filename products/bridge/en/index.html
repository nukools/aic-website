How it works

One box, four ways to get data

AIC Bridge uses the best method for each machine. New machines are read from the controller; old machines with nothing to connect to are measured through electrical current and sensors.

FAQ

FAQ

Do I need to change the existing PLC program?

No. AIC Bridge is read-only by default. If a machine does not allow data access, it can measure electrical current or use separate sensors without touching the existing control system.

Does it work on very old machines without a PLC?

Yes. Clamp a CT on the power cable to get machine status and cycles, or take the signal from the status light, and add analog sensors for values such as pressure or temperature.

Does it need a constant internet connection?

No. The box has a built-in dashboard you can use on your local network. The optional cloud service adds remote mobile access, LINE alerts and multi-site views.

Can I install it myself, or does your team need to?

It is designed for self-install with an illustrated guide, but work inside electrical panels should be done by a licensed electrician. A.I.C. and our partners can install it for you.

Which machine and inverter brands are supported?

Most devices that support Modbus or OPC UA connect right away, and we are adding ready-made templates for popular models. Tell us your brands and models in the form and we will prioritize them.

Do I have to pay to reserve?

No. Reserving is free with no commitment. When the first batch goes on sale, reservation holders get the special price first.
